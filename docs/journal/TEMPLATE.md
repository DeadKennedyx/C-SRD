# YYYY-MM-DD — short research question

Status: proposed / running / completed / inconclusive / superseded.
Stage: discovery / definition / solution search / specification / implementation / analysis.
Observation type: contemporaneous / retrospective / proposed.
Related specification, task and commit:

## Thinking, doing and questions

* What am I thinking?
* What am I doing?
* What do I want to do?
* What do I want to know?
* What did I discover? Leave this unanswered until observations exist.

## Initial observations and background

Record the symptom, environment and historical context. Link raw logs/reports,
background sources, assumptions and initial questions. Separate observations
from interpretations. Record alternative explanations, including likely causes
that later prove irrelevant.

## Problem and hypothesis

State one specific problem in one sentence. State a falsifiable hypothesis and
its domain. Identify constraints, competing explanations and what evidence
would reject or leave the hypothesis unresolved.

## Experimental design, protocol and materials

Record experimental units, treatments, controls, factors, nuisance variables,
input/corpus selection, seeds, versions, commands, bounds, repetitions and output
locations. Specify metrics, denominators, planned analysis and decision rules
before running. Explain any manual challenge or selection bias. If retrospective,
say so explicitly instead of presenting these as prospective choices.

## Implementation and deviations

Record code/specification changes, problems encountered and deviations from the
planned protocol. Explain which prior assumption needs reconsideration and which
tests distinguish the alternative explanations. Do not fabricate chronology.

## Data, analysis and decision

Link raw results and exact artifact identities. Keep tested, bounded-checked,
proved-under-model, unsupported and inconclusive distinct. Describe uncertainty,
counterexamples, false matches/misses and effects of confounding variables.
Decision: accept within stated scope / reject / inconclusive / revise protocol.

## Reflection and handoff

What did the evidence change? What remains unknown? What should be preserved,
repeated or tried next? State the next task, dependencies and reproducible entry
point. Record an owner/date only when one has actually been assigned.
